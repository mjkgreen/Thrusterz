Privacy

Thrusterz · last updated 9 October 2026

Thrusterz is a game. It has no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics, and it never sells or shares your data.

What stays on your device

Your stars, best results and settings are saved on your device so the game works offline.

What the online pilot account stores

Online features (cloud save, leaderboards) use a pilot account that the game creates the first time you use them. Our server stores:

  • a random account ID and restore code, and the hashed session keys of your devices;
  • the pilot name you choose, shown on leaderboards;
  • your game progress (stars, best Δv, scores);
  • your best leaderboard runs: the recorded controls of the flight, used to verify the score;
  • if you use Sign in with Apple, the anonymous user identifier Apple gives us. We never see your Apple ID email or name.

We use this only to run those features. Server logs (such as IP addresses) are kept briefly by our hosting provider for security and rate limiting.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Pilot → Delete account. This removes your account, name, leaderboard entries and cloud save from our server, and revokes Sign in with Apple. Progress saved on your device stays until you delete the app.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information beyond what is listed above.
